One transition for every screen

Each change of screen gets its own animation, built from what is on that screen. The rule you set: when the new screen is a quiet one (prayer in progress, night, Jumu'ah, khutbah) the animation is muted: a slow cross-fade. Everything else gets the lively version. It is the real board in the frames, and the animation moves the board's real elements. Not wired into the app.   Earlier pages: simple · wild.

Chains: main → azan → between → iqamah → prayer → after → main. There is a short blank between steps while the next screen loads; that is the mock, not the animation.
